Caroline Coolidge

About

Caroline Coolidge is an interdisciplinary artist based between New York City and Edinburgh, Scotland. Her practice is rooted in printmaking and moves between painting, print, and installation, tracing the fragile space between image and information. She is interested in what happens when an image is pushed beyond visibility—when scale, repetition, and saturation cause information to dissolve into abstraction. Within the cosmology of her work, the screen serves as both material and metaphor, shaping her ongoing investigation into perception, recognition, and that which is lost in translation. She occupies the shifting space between painting and printmaking, treating the two as overlapping realities.

Coolidge’s work has been exhibited internationally at venues including Patriothall Gallery in Edinburgh, the Dolphin Gallery at St John’s College at the University of Oxford, Spoke Gallery in Boston, and the Carpenter Center for the Visual Arts at Harvard University. She has been an artist-in-residence at the Vermont Studio Center and Ghost Ranch in New Mexico, held a Curatorial Research Fellowship at the MIT List Visual Arts Center, and worked as a teaching fellow in the Art, Film, and Visual Studies department at Harvard University. Coolidge is currently pursuing an MFA at Columbia University, where she is a Neiman Fellow. She holds degrees from Harvard University and the Ruskin School of Art at the University of Oxford.
